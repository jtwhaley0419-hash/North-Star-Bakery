About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery is built around a simple idea: good food brings neighbors together. We focus on handmade baking and a friendly place to stop during a busy day.

How we choose ingredients

We value fresh ingredients, thoughtful recipes, and small batches. We aim to work with local suppliers when practical and to keep our baking consistent.

Meet the people behind the counter

Our bakers prepare the day's loaves and pastries, while our front-counter team helps customers choose treats and plan orders for special occasions.
